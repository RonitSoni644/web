What These Price Ranges Mean

Basic Website

₹5,000 – ₹20,000

  • Simple 3–5 pages
  • Domain + hosting + basic design
  • Good for personal sites or small informational pages
  • Minimal coding — often template-based

Small Business Website

₹20,000 – ₹80,000

  • WordPress or CMS
  • Responsive layout, contact forms, galleries/blog
  • Most local business sites fall here

Medium Business Website

₹80,000 – ₹2,50,000

  • Custom design, more pages/features
  • Better SEO & visuals
  • Stronger branding and user experience

E-Commerce Website

₹50,000 – ₹3,00,000+

  • Product catalog, shopping cart & checkout
  • Payment gateway integrations
  • Costs rise with product count and custom features

Custom / Enterprise Website

₹2,00,000 – ₹10,00,000+

  • Fully custom UX/UI, web apps, dashboards
  • APIs and backend systems
  • For large businesses or complex platforms

Recurring Costs to Remember

  • Domain renewal: ₹500 – ₹2,000/year
  • Hosting renewal: ₹1,500 – ₹50,000+/year
  • Maintenance & security: ₹5,000 – ₹50,000+/year

How These Costs Work Together

1. Domain Name – ₹500 – ₹1,000/year
Your web address; .in domains ~₹500–₹800/yr, .com ~₹800–₹1,200/yr. HTBG Global

2. Web Hosting – ₹1,500 – ₹2,000/year
Shared hosting keeps small sites online and often includes free SSL. HTBG Global

3. SSL Certificate – Free – ₹500/year
Many hosts include SSL; otherwise a basic SSL costs ~₹500/yr. Claritussolutions

4. Website Software / Platform – ₹0
Use free WordPress + theme or free builder plans for no software cost. Claritussolutions

5. Website Setup / Design
DIY is free; freelancer setup starts ~₹5,000–₹10,000+. HTBG Global

Tips to Save Money

  • Use free themes & plugins on WordPress. Hostinger
  • Buy domain and hosting from the same provider — sometimes cheaper. Scintilla Web
  • Avoid premium tools until you need them.

What You Don’t Need Initially: Expensive custom design, advanced SEO services, e-commerce features for a basic site.

Explanation — What These Costs Include

Domain & Hosting
Your domain name (website address) — usually ₹500 – ₹2,000 per year. Hosting — space on a server that makes your site live online. Shared hosting (cheaper) is ₹1,500 – ₹5,000/year. Claritussolutions

SSL certificate
Often free with hosting or ₹1,000 – ₹5,000/year. Claritussolutions

Examples
Personal site: ₹5,000 – ₹20,000. Local business site: ₹20,000 – ₹75,000. Full business site with SEO: ₹50,000 – ₹1,20,000. Small online store: ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000.
